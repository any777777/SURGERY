# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Medical students using a phone first, often in short review sessions between classes, rounds, or exam preparation blocks. They need to answer surgery MCQs, see the correct answer immediately after committing, and understand the explanation without losing their place.

## Product Purpose

Turn the source surgery MCQ book into a complete study platform. Success means every extractable question, option, answer, section, explanation, and useful figure from the book is available in a fast, searchable, mobile-friendly study experience. The app should help students practice, review mistakes, reinforce recall, and revisit explanations by topic.

## Brand Personality

Clinical calm, academic, practical. The product should feel like a serious medical study companion: restrained, precise, readable, and focused on learning rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Do not use AI-looking visual tropes: decorative gradients, glass panels, oversized marketing heroes, noisy card grids, novelty illustrations, or purple SaaS aesthetics. Avoid a landing-page feel. Avoid dense textbook walls that are hard to use on a phone. Avoid anything that makes the student hunt for the next question, answer, or explanation.

## Design Principles

- Practice first: the first screen should lead directly into studying, not marketing.
- Preserve the source: book-derived content is primary, and external enrichment must be clearly distinguishable.
- Commit before reveal: students answer first, then see correctness, rationale, and review actions.
- Mobile clinical workflow: controls, reading widths, touch targets, and navigation must work comfortably on a phone.
- Calm density: show enough academic information to be useful without visually exhausting the learner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Use high contrast body text, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, reduced-motion support, readable line lengths, and touch targets suitable for one-handed mobile use. Avoid relying on color alone for correct/incorrect feedback.
